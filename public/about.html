🏡 Our Story

Built from the inside out.

FamilyCrate wasn't sketched on a whiteboard or pitched to investors first. It was born in a real kitchen, for a real family, built around what actually works — and what actually lasts.

The moment it clicked — everyone on the same page, without being told.
The Origin

It started with a ridiculous Tuesday morning.

We homeschool. We have three kids at three completely different stages of life. Between school schedules, activities, chores, and just keeping the house from descending into total chaos — the weight of managing it all lands somewhere, usually on Mom and Dad.

I'm pretty on top of things, tech-wise. So when I heard about an app called The Chores App, I was genuinely excited. I found it, went to download it — and our iPad was too old to run it. An iPad. Too old. For a chore app.

That was the moment. Not anger — just clarity. This shouldn't be this hard. And whatever I built would need to run on any device, no installs, no hardware requirements, no nonsense.

The Search

Every app had something missing.

Before building anything, I did what any reasonable dad does — I tried everything else first.

📋
FamilyWall
Lists, yes. Points? No. Where's the fun in that for kids?
💡
Skylight
Great design — and a beautiful price tag to match. But after hearing firsthand from a few moms about its hiccups, I kept looking.
📱
The Chores App
Required a newer iPad. Then required connecting to Sheets for the right view. That was enough.
🔗
Everything else
Made for iPhones. Which kids don't have. Which defeats the whole point.
"I showed FamilyCrate to a mom friend early in development. She went jaw-dropped. That's when I knew we had something."
— Cliff, founder & dad of three
The Discovery

We didn't need more features.
We needed the right ones.

Once I started building, the requirements became clear fast. Not from market research — from actually living it.

01
Works on any device, instantly
A web app. No download. No age requirement. Opens on the family iPad, a phone, a laptop — same experience everywhere.
02
Kids actually want to use it
Points, leaderboards, and a real rewards store. Not gold stars. Something they work toward — and something parents actually deliver.
03
One login. The whole family.
No separate kid accounts. No asking Mom or Dad to unlock something. Kids tap their name, check their stuff, done.
04
Parents stay in control — without effort
A PIN-protected parent layer. Approve rewards, manage chores, connect Google Calendar. Depth without complexity.

But here's what we didn't expect — and what the research backs up: the rewards that work best aren't things. They're experiences. Adventures. Time together. A late-night movie, a spontaneous trip, a day that's just theirs. When kids work toward something meaningful, something they actually remember, the system stops being about chores and starts being about character. Follow-through. Accountability. Doing what you said you'd do.

FamilyCrate isn't an allowance app. It's a framework for raising kids who do hard things — not because they were told to, but because they learned that effort leads somewhere worth going. Parents who show up and deliver on those rewards aren't just keeping a promise. They're building trust, one redeemed point at a time.

They check it themselves.
Without being asked.

That's the thing we didn't expect — once kids have something to work toward, the reminders stop. The chores just… get done.

What's Inside

Everything a family actually needs.
Nothing they don't.

It's been running in our house every single day. These aren't features we guessed at — they're the ones that changed how our family operates.

📅
Family Calendar
Day and week views. Each person has their own color-coded column. Google Calendar syncs in. Everyone sees exactly what's happening and when.
✅
Chores & To-Dos
Assign recurring chores with time slots, point values, and due dates. Kids check them off themselves. No spreadsheets, no charts, no nagging.
🏆
Points & Leaderboard
Every completed chore earns points. The family leaderboard creates natural sibling motivation — without a parent saying a word.
🎁
Rewards Store
Kids redeem points for real rewards you define — screen time, outings, special treats. Parents approve. Everyone wins.
🛒
Grocery & Lists
A shared grocery list that lives next to everything else. Add items, assign them, check them off. One less thing to text about.
🔒
Parent Controls
A PIN-protected layer for parents. Manage members, set reward periods, approve redemptions, and connect your calendar — all hidden from kids.
The Shift

From nagging to nudging.

Here's what surprised us most. Once FamilyCrate was running in our house, the conversations around chores changed completely. Instead of a reminder that felt like a correction, it became something lighter:

"Hey, I just added a few things to the board — looks like there are some good points up for grabs today."

That's it. That's the whole thing. The system does the heavy lifting. Parents get to be the fun ones again.
