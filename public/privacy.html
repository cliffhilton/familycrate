Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

The short version: FamilyCrate is a family app. We collect only what we need to make it work. We never sell your data. Your family's information stays private and secure.

1. Who We Are

FamilyCrate ("we," "us," or "our") is a family organization platform operated by FamilyCrate, LLC. We provide a web-based application for families to manage calendars, chores, points, and rewards at familycrate.co.

For privacy questions, contact us at hello@familycrate.co.

2. Information We Collect

Account Data

When you register

  • Parent/guardian email address and password (hashed, never stored in plain text)
  • Family name
  • Family member names and optional profile photos
  • Optional email addresses for family members (for notifications only)
Usage Data

When you use the app

  • Calendar events and schedules you create
  • Chores, tasks, and to-do items you add
  • Points earned and rewards redeemed
  • Completed task records (done log)
  • Reward store items and redemption requests
  • App settings and preferences (point values, period lengths, list categories)
Payment Data

When you subscribe

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your credit card number, CVV, or full payment details. We receive only a subscription status and customer ID from Stripe.

What We Do NOT Collect
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect device identifiers or fingerprints
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics pixels
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing
  • We do not read your calendar events for any purpose other than displaying them to your family

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

FamilyCrate is designed for families and may be used by children under 13. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • Account control: All accounts are created and controlled by a parent or guardian. Children do not register independently.
  • No direct collection from children: We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Child profiles (names, avatars) are created and managed by the registering parent.
  • Parental access: Parents can view, edit, or delete all family member data at any time from the Settings screen.
  • No behavioral advertising: We never use any family member's data for targeted advertising.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information without parental consent, please contact us at hello@familycrate.co and we will delete it promptly.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the FamilyCrate app
  • To process your subscription payment via Stripe
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, trial reminders) via Resend
  • To display your family's data across devices in real time
  • To maintain and improve the service

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating FamilyCrate.

5. Data Storage and Security

Encrypted at Rest & in Transit

Database — Supabase (PostgreSQL)

All family data is stored in Supabase, a managed PostgreSQL platform hosted on AWS. Supabase provides:

  • AES-256 encryption at rest for all stored data
  • TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit
  • Row Level Security (RLS) — database policies ensure each family can only access their own data
  • SOC 2 Type 2 compliant infrastructure
Frontend Security

In the browser / app

  • Authentication tokens are stored in browser localStorage and sent via HTTPS only
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage — we never store plain text passwords
  • All API requests require a valid Bearer token — unauthenticated requests are rejected
  • Parent PIN is stored locally on the device only — it never leaves your device
Backend Security

Server infrastructure — Railway

  • Our server runs on Railway, a managed cloud platform
  • All traffic is served over HTTPS with TLS certificates managed automatically
  • API keys and secrets are stored as encrypted environment variables, never in code
  • The server uses a Supabase service key that bypasses RLS only for authenticated, authorized requests
  • Stripe webhook signatures are verified on every payment event

6. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following service providers, strictly to operate FamilyCrate:

  • Supabase — database hosting (AWS infrastructure)
  • Railway — application server hosting
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Resend — transactional email delivery

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

7. Data Retention

We retain your family data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription:

  • Your data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation
  • After 30 days, your account and all associated data may be permanently deleted
  • You may request immediate deletion at any time by emailing hello@familycrate.co

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your family's data
  • Correction — update any incorrect information (directly in the app)
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all data
  • Portability — request an export of your data in JSON format

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@familycrate.co.

9. Cookies

FamilyCrate does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage to store your authentication token and app preferences. No third-party cookies are set.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or by posting a notice in the app. Continued use of FamilyCrate after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
